Behind every story

A creative process, held with care.

There is no single right way to begin. Facilitators help each storyteller move at their own pace and shape a story that feels true to them.

01 · Reflect

Find the moment

Look back at the people, places, and turning points that shaped your perspective.

02 · Shape

Build the narrative

Develop a focused script and discover the details that make the story your own.

03 · Create

Bring it to life

Pair your words with images, voice, and sound to create a short digital story.
